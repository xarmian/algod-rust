## 1. Risk Governance & Scoring Framework

This registry tracks all strategic, architectural, cryptographic, consensus, and operational risks affecting `algod-rust`. It is divided into two distinct governance categories:

1. **Risks We Can Influence (Controllable / Internal Risks — `RISK-INT-NNN`):**
   Risks originating within the `algod-rust` codebase, architecture, build pipeline, dependency selection, test harnesses, or node configuration defaults. Engineering and security teams have direct control to eliminate, mitigate, or bound these risks through code changes, differential testing, fuzzing, and formal verification.
2. **Risks We Cannot Influence (Uncontrollable / External & Systemic Risks — `RISK-EXT-NNN`):**
   Risks originating outside `algod-rust`'s control boundary — including upstream Algorand protocol design choices, MainNet stake distribution, cryptographic mathematical breakthroughs, zero-day vulnerabilities in upstream `go-algorand` that `algod-rust` must remain bug-for-bug compatible with to avoid chain splits, OS/hardware faults, and global network routing attacks. While we cannot eliminate their root causes, we document **defensive posture, monitoring, and contingency plans** where possible.

### Risk Rating Scale
- **Inherent Risk:** Risk level before engineering controls (`Critical` / `High` / `Medium` / `Low`).
- **Residual Risk:** Risk level after current controls and verification (`Critical` / `High` / `Medium` / `Low` / `Accepted`).
- **Status:** `Active` | `Mitigated` | `Monitoring` | `Accepted`

These risks lie outside the `algod-rust` repository boundary. We cannot patch them away in isolation without breaking Algorand network compatibility, or they depend on external physical/economic realities.
